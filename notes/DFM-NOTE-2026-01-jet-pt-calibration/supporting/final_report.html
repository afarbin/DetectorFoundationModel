F10 - the representations transfer; the gains are where theory says they should be. The linear probe result is the standout: a completely frozen pretrained encoder + a trained head matches full end-to-end training (JER 0.157 vs 0.158, and the best closure of the family) - the masked-cell pretext, trained with zero jet labels on different physics samples, already encodes essentially everything the cells-only calibration uses. With labels abundant, finetuning from it neither helps nor hurts; at 1-10% labels the pretrained init is consistently ~1-2% relatively better (modest, same-direction across fractions, ~1σ/point with 3 seeds).
Honest caveats: label-efficiency gains are small at this model scale (~1M params) and the pretrain/finetune corpus overlap is unverifiable for ttbar_1000 (EventNumber unfilled) - though the 3-corpus mix dilutes that concern. The stronger claim is the probe, which is unaffected by overlap in its interpretation as representation quality.

4. Operations record

Campaign survived: one node freeze requiring an admin reboot (root cause identified: uncapped torch/numpy OpenMP pools - every process defaulted to all 56 cores; now capped at 6), /tmp wipe (code moved to /storage), two dataloader crash modes (pin-memory on custom containers; fd-exhaustion under concurrent workers - both fixed), and a VPN outage. Guardrails now standing: resource watchdog (alert / pause / kill tiers), niced trainings, SSH multiplexing, reboot-proof re-entrant queues. GPU 1 was excluded per Mohammad Ali's running job; a memory diagnostic for his 186 GB trainer was written to reviews/trainer_memory_diagnostic.md.

5. What's next

  • Stage 2 (event-level): the isolated→non-isolated gap is measured and waiting; jets negotiating shared energy via query self-attention is the designed follow-up, and the D1 flags make the dataset ready for it.
  • Closure polish: numerical inversion (or MAE-final) for the 20-30 GeV bin before any external-facing numbers.
  • Flavor: b/c jets are in the shards (labels kept) - flavor-dependent calibration is a flag away.
  • Pretraining at scale: the probe says the pretext works; the lever now is corpus size and a bigger encoder, not protocol.
