Jet reconstruction as a global correction:
one detector backbone, many tasks

Mechanics and results: jet energy, flavor, joint tagging+calibration, missing ET, and set-prediction jet finding — on HL-LHC full simulation, with the evidence for each claim.

Amir Farbin (UTA) · with Umar Sohail Qureshi (SLAC) and Mohammadali Ghaznavi

Approach

Compensate the sources of hadronic shower fluctuations, globally

  • JER is limited by per-jet fluctuations — charged fraction, shower composition, out-of-cluster losses, pileup — that average-based calibration cannot remove.
  • We train networks on a jet's own constituents to predict a per-jet multiplicative correction: target y = ln(p_T^truth / p_T^reco), so p_T^pred = p_T^reco · e^ŷ. Heteroscedastic heads emit (μ, σ) — an uncertainty per jet.
  • This is a global correction — all effects compensated simultaneously, avoiding particle-flow's per-constituent confusion term. The modern extension of the Global Sequential Calibration; complementary to p-flow.
  • Targets are strictly particle-level (matched truth jets). Parton-level/out-of-cone effects are process physics, not calibration.
Read every result with this in mind: the target is a correction on top of the calorimeter jet. "Tracks only" means "tracks correcting the calo jet", not a track-based measurement. Every table therefore starts from the uncorrected calo-jet row.
Data & mechanics I

Sample: HL-LHC Run-4 full simulation, provenance pinned

  • mc21_14TeV.601229 Powheg+Pythia8 A14 ttbar (hdamp 1.5mt), single-lepton filter; AOD e8481_s4446_r16176 — full Geant4, geometry ATLAS-P2-RUN4-03-01-00 (ITk). Traced through the PanDA task record (task 50733453).
  • We hold 17 ntuple files = 340k events → 1.24M selected jets (0.6% of a 44.7M-event production; more being transferred).
  • Per jet: cone (ΔR<0.4) calorimeter cells with their calo-geometry neighbor graph, ghost-associated tracks (18 features incl. IP significances from covariances), jet kinematics, matched truth jet.

Selection (cutflow archived per file): truth-matched, pTtrue>20 GeV, ΔR(reco,truth)<0.3, |η|<2.5, duplicate-truth guard; isolation stored as flags and used to define evaluation populations.

Known dataset caveats (measured, not assumed):
  • Cells are the ntuple's thinned subset (|E/σ|>2): ~75% energy retention, layer-biased → cell results are a lower bound.
  • 5.3% of matched jets overlap a truth electron (pT>10, ΔR<0.2) — the SingleLep lepton. In "light" for these results; veto planned.
  • Truth jets include neutrinos (measured: ν-in-cone jets' stable-sum/truth-pT = 0.966 with ν, 0.731 without).
Data & mechanics II

One shared encoder; inputs are the experiment

SharedEventEncoder: constituents as typed tokens with per-modality input projections + type embeddings; local mixing per modality (cells: SAGE-style convolution over the fixed calo-neighbor graph, or set-attention; tracks: k-NN EdgeConv); global mask-aware ISAB attention, cross-modality; per-jet queries cross-attend and pool → (μ, log σ²) head trained with Gaussian NLL.

Config grammar = input ablation: subsets of {T racks, J et kinematics, C ells} × cell encoding {graph, set} — same encoder, same training, only inputs differ. dim 128, ~1M params.

Training: AdamW, flat-pT reweighting on train; normalization fit on train only; train on isolated jets, evaluate both populations; 3 seeds per config (all numbers shown = mean ± sd over seeds).

Estimators (both reported): robust JER = IQR/1.349/median in truth-pT bins; ATLAS-convention iterative Gaussian-core fits for cross-checks. Closure = RMS of (median corrected response − 1) over 20–250 GeV bins. Pull std tests the σ's.

Honesty budget 3 seeds is thin — spreads are shown everywhere; a 10-seed re-run under a pre-registered protocol is in progress. Nothing below hides behind an inclusive average.

Result 2 — flavor dependence (all-flavor dataset, 1.2M jets)

Shared training beats dedicated per-flavor models

What the evidence actually supports:

  • One shared model beats dedicated per-flavor models where statistics are limited: b: 12.8±0.1 vs 13.9; c: 13.3±0.2 vs 15.4±0.7 — the dedicated c-model starves on 150k jets.
  • Explicit flavor conditioning adds little over flavor-blind: b 12.8±0.1 vs 13.0±0.03; c consistent within errors. The encoder evidently infers flavor-relevant structure from the constituents themselves.
  • Flavor ordering (light < b < c) matches expectation: semileptonic decays and shower composition degrade b/c resolution.

Do the per-jet uncertainties mean anything?

Validation beyond pull std — first diagnostics

Interpretation: the σ's are honest in the core (pull std ≈ 1.0–1.04 everywhere) but the residual distribution has non-Gaussian tails a single-Gaussian head cannot represent. This is measured motivation — not speculation — for the mixture-density head study now starting (does a second component absorb the tails, and does it align with the semileptonic-b population?).

Limitations — stated before anyone asks

What these results are not

  • All calibrations are corrections to the calo jet — no standalone single-modality measurement is claimed. (A constituents-only absolute-pT variant is planned to measure standalone information content.)
  • 3 seeds per config; spreads shown, but headline configs are being re-run at 10 seeds under a pre-registered protocol with frozen splits and test blinding.
  • Thinned cells (~75% energy retention, layer-biased): every cell-based number is a lower bound; full-granularity same-event data requires a new ntuple production.
  • Single physics process (ttbar) — event-level models can exploit ttbar kinematics; sample-dependence unmeasured until the VBF H→inv sample (in transfer) and ideally dijet/Z+jets arrive.
  • Electron contamination: 5.3% of "light" jets overlap the hard lepton (measured); veto in progress — small shifts in light-jet numbers expected.
  • Pretraining verdict is modest and honestly reported: neutral at full labels; the gain lives at 1–10% labels (see ladder table) — the foundation-model claim will be staked there, with margins registered in advance.
  • No production-tagger baseline (GN2 branches unfilled) and no reco-MET soft term — internal comparisons only where noted.
