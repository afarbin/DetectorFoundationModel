Two of Ariel's questions, answered with data

Neutrinos are in the truth jets; electrons are in our jet sample

"I don't think neutrinos are included in particle jets" Measured (2k events, jets pT>30, |η|<2.5): for jets with a ν (pT>3) in cone, stable-particle cone sum / TruthHSJet pT:

0.966 with ν  ·  0.731 without

→ this production's truth jets include neutrinos. The target carries the invisible energy; the semileptonic-b "network predicts the ν loss" mechanism stands.
"You did not consider jets matched to electrons, right?" Measured: 5.3% of matched reco jets have a truth electron (pT>10) within ΔR<0.2 — the SingleLep lepton faking a jet.

Present in v1 "light" (and plausibly in the SLAC sample too). v2: sidecar electron veto, rate reported, difference documented.

Both results are one-command reproducible from the QA scripts; full-statistics versions land in the v2 datasheet.

Ariel's review → program changes (all committed)

Framing and conventions adopted

  • Physics stance: not "ML jet calibration" — a global correction compensating the sources of hadronic shower fluctuations; modern GSC extension, complementary to p-flow (no per-constituent confusion term); strictly particle-level targets.
  • MPV (Gaussian-core μ) as the response figure of merit; median kept as robust cross-check.
  • 30 GeV reporting floor (HL-LHC convention) — also retires our floor-clipped 20–30 bin.
  • Feature-ablation ladder: baseline = jet (pT, η, φ) only → +GSC-like engineered → +track relatives → +cells → +hits → +timing; each step's ΔJER with CIs. Physics-motivated, not kitchen-sink.
  • Failure-mode program: event displays + feature distributions for extreme-correction jets; first target is the topocluster-threshold boundary mechanism.
  • N/S/C reported with parameter correlations, framed as preliminary/descriptive.
  • Open scope decision: |η|>2.5 (needs HGTD-aware handling + rebuild) — propose deferring to v2.1.
